Arrhenius analysis

Combine rate constants from experiments at different temperatures to estimate the activation energy Eₐ and pre-exponential factor A.

#TemperatureRate constant k
EXAMPLE DATASET

Add at least 2 (Temperature, k) pairs from separate runs of the same reaction — 3 or more gives a meaningful R². "Use current result's k" grabs the selected fit's k from the Results page and the Temperature field from the Data page.

Arrhenius equation: k = A·exp(−Eₐ / RT), linearized as ln k = ln A − (Eₐ/R)(1/T). Plotting ln k vs 1/T gives a line of slope −Eₐ/R and intercept ln A.
